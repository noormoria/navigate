What does the skill strength mean? It measures how important that skill is to the company if this person leaves — not how skilled the person is.

1 · LowLimited business impact and relatively easy to replace.
2 · ModerateUseful skill, but the company can usually replace or cover it.
3 · StrongImportant to the role or team and its loss would be noticeable.
4 · Very strongHard to replace and has high business impact.
5 · CriticalRare or core skill; losing it could strongly affect operations or results.
