ABOUT NAVIGATE

A clearer way to review customer retention risk.

NAVIGATE combines customer-retention analysis with practical decision-support tools, including churn review, saved history, and structured CV comparison against role criteria.

01

Measure

Capture six observable customer signals: tenure, satisfaction, activity frequency, total spend, recency, and complaints.

02

Estimate

A calibrated model estimates churn probability and compares it with a validation-selected decision threshold.

03

Act

Translate the score into a clearer review of customer value, retention priority, signals, and next actions.

WHY CHURN MATTERS

Customer loss changes growth, revenue stability, and the cost of maintaining a healthy customer base.

01

Risk can appear before departure

Lower satisfaction, longer inactivity, complaints, or weaker engagement can provide signals worth reviewing before a customer leaves.

02

Risk should be reviewed with value

Not every at-risk customer has the same business value, so risk and value should be reviewed together.

03

Earlier visibility supports better decisions

Structured review helps teams move from reactive retention to earlier, more consistent follow-up.

MODEL TRANSPARENCY

What NAVIGATE currently is — and is not.

The current model is trained on an e-commerce churn dataset using six behavior features selected because they can be interpreted across many customer relationships. It is a decision-support prototype, not a universally validated model for every industry.

Customer identity and contact information are stored only for the signed-in user's history and are not model inputs.
